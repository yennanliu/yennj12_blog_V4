## Batch summary

Three of the four series are **repo tours with a thesis**, not repo tours without one, and they are the strongest cluster the audit has seen in the agent space: QM ("design for many people first, then ask how they share one agent") and OpenWorker ("the harness is 30× the loop; what matters is what the agent is *not* allowed to decide") quote real code, pin a commit, and almost every repo fact I checked is right — `AGENTS.md` phrases, `.npmrc min-release-age=7`, the "speed bump" sentence, the three portal-only actions, `engine.py`'s functions and `max_iterations = 12`. The author's own `agent_auto_system` series is lighter (259–347 lines) but every PR number/title and README figure (11 task types, 380 tests, ~450 MB, 0.5 s SSE poll, WeasyPrint) checks out. The consultant series is the weak quarter: five posts with no measured numbers, a `langgraph.graph import interrupt` that does not exist, a LINE handler that `NameError`s on `MessageEvent`, a graph whose "parallel" node is never wired, and an estimate example whose line items do not recompute from its own hourly rates. Interview-format leakage is limited to the 「為什麼選 X 不選 Y」 table (all 10 QM/OpenWorker posts, 6–15 rows each, with "反轉條件: 沒有" in ~30% of rows); no post has 三個演進階段, and the one 「系統效應」 table (auto pt.2 §八) is qualitative. Only one "實測" label sits on an unsourced figure (openworker pt.2:434 "4.5 s → 1.2 s"). A drift point: QM's `SECURITY.md` now lists **14** known limitations, not the 12 the posts enumerate. Verdicts: **Ready 13** (auto pt.1–4, qm pt.1–5, openworker pt.1, 3, 4, 5), **Needs revision 7** (auto pt.5, openworker pt.2, consultant pt.1–5), **Not ready 0**.

## Recommendations

2. **Recompute the consultant pt.4 estimate from its own rate card and keep one quoted range.** WHY: a reader will paste this into a real proposal; 68,800 vs 64,800 is a 6% error they inherit. HOW: a 10-line Python cell in the post that multiplies L302–304 rates by L316–326 hours; replace L591/L649 with the same range.

HOW: one paragraph after the pinned-commit footer in pt.1 of each series listing what changed, refreshed when the series is next touched; keep the enumerations as dated.
4. **Replace the single "實測" in openworker pt.2 with a measurement or a derivation.** WHY: it is the one figure in 12,000 lines that pretends to be data; the rest of the batch is honest, and this undercuts it. HOW: run `explore` with 5 greps under `_parallel_safe` on/off and paste the timings, or restate as the obvious bound (wall time ≈ slowest call).
