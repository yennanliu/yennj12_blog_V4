## Batch summary

The batch has a consistent and good architectural core — every post picks one real tension (pseudonymise before the
LLM, trace-first diagnosis, hedged requests vs gather, envelope encryption vs latency) and explains the mechanism
with diagrams that carry information. Clarity and visuals score 4–5 almost everywhere. Accuracy is where the batch
fails, and it fails in a specific, repeatable way: numbers that look sourced are not, and the same quantity is
stated two or three times with different values inside one post (Phase section vs 系統效應 table vs 面試答題要點).
Eight posts have a derived number that does not survive recomputation or contradicts the post's own inputs: part 43's
"500,000 Workers" (6M requests ÷ 12 s, dimensionally meaningless), part 44's Phase 3 at both $15.03M and $3.58M/month,
part 46's cost table that concludes "BYOK 實際更便宜" while its own rows say $115K vs $51K, part 47's cloud cost that
multiplies users instead of queries (50× off), part 49's $8/month Firestore blacklist that would really cost
~$30K/month at the stated QPS, part 51's worked example whose sessions (30 × 400 = 12K tokens) can never reach the
32K cache threshold it is built on, part 52's $150/month for 1.5M API calls at $0.001. Three posts contain a
mechanism a reader would implement wrongly: part 45 says NFC normalisation defeats full-width homoglyphs (it does
not — NFKC does), part 47's entropy threshold H > 2.5 is unreachable with top-5 logprobs (max log2 5 = 2.32), part
46's `--rotation-period 3600s` is below Cloud KMS's one-day minimum and its "DEK version mismatch" failure is not how
KMS CMEK behaves (old key versions stay decryptable). Two posts have invented APIs (part 43's
`update_state(skip_nodes_before=…)` and `"__next__"` injection, neither in LangGraph). Format problems are real too:
part 45's source contains a literal NUL byte and C0 control characters inside a regex, so `file` reports it as
`data` and `grep` treats it as binary; part 51 has a blockquote dropped into the middle of its 系統效應 table, which
splits the table on render. The honest caveat boxes that 44, 46, 47, 49 and 51 added later ("Vertex AI is ScaNN not
HNSW", "managed Gemini has no VRAM", "the 12 s number is a deliberately exaggerated strawman") are the best writing
in the batch — but each sits on top of a body that was not revised to match it. Verdicts: **0 Ready**,
**7 Needs revision** (40, 41, 42, 44, 48, 50, 52), **6 Not ready** (43, 45, 46, 47, 49, 51).

## Patterns

### Content quality
- Thesis is clear in every post and the opening 4-line contrast is consistently the strongest paragraph (45:13–16, 47:13–16, 52:13–16). The problem is never "what is the idea" but "which of the three numbers for it is true".
- The later-added caveat boxes are excellent and should become the model: 44:448 (customers cannot see TPU load), 46:72 (the 12 s path is an exaggerated strawman; managed Vector Search needs EKM/KAJ policy, not per-query decrypt), 47:455–458 (managed Gemini has no instances to pre-warm; use Provisioned Throughput), 49:20 (Vertex AI is ScaNN), 51:21 (managed Gemini has no KV cache). But none of the bodies were rewritten: 46's Phase 3 diagram still decrypts Vector Search in an enclave (46:253–262), 47 still argues TCP RST in 八 (47:610), 49 still tunes `ef_search` on Vertex AI (49:740), 51's 面試情境 still pairs a Vertex AI bill with GPU OOM (51:19–20).
- 42 overlaps almost entirely with `fde-core-concept-21/23/24/25` and part 27/29 (discovery → constraints, stakeholder map, POC scoring, value story); 45 with part 13/20 and `fde-core-concept-6/7`; 46 with `fde-core-concept-9/10`; 49 with `fde-core-concept-15`; 50 with `fde-core-concept-19/20`; 52 with `fde-core-concept-14`. Each pair restates the same decision table.

### Structure (does the house format help or pad?)
- 三個演進階段 helps when the phases add a distinct mechanism (43: MemorySaver → MongoDBSaver → Spanner CAS; 45: single prompt → split services → dual-model) and pads when they only change adjectives (42:49–108 "Discovery → Assessment → Proposal" is a sales funnel; 41:63–130 is three tiers of the same dashboard).
- The 為什麼選 X 不選 Y tables are *not* verbatim repeats of earlier sections; flip conditions exist in all 10 tabular posts and are usually specific. The padding is elsewhere: every post ends with a 系統效應 table of 10–12 invented rows (43:723–737, 47:592–604, 49:651–664) that re-states the Phase numbers with a third set of values, and then a 面試答題要點 that re-states both. 44 adds 延伸問題 (44:815), 45 adds 延伸思考 + 總結 (45:587, 606), 48 adds 十一 追問 (48:664), 49 adds 十一/十二/十三 (49:655, 739, 812 — 十三 is a "系列回顧" that praises the series), 52 adds 延伸閱讀. The section cap is exceeded in 11 of 13.
- Heading hygiene broke under the extra sections: 44 numbers subsections 7b.1–7b.4 under 八, 8.1–8.2 under 九, 9.1–9.2 and 11.3 under 十一; 46 and 51 have a bare `## 系列導航` heading followed by a second `**系列導航**` (46:747–749, 51:808–812).
- 43–52 all carry the same timestamp (2026-06-08T09:00:00) and 40–42 are 10:00–12:00, so date-sorted lists show 40–42 *after* 43–52; 43/45/46/48/50/51/52 switch the title style to "FDE 面試指南 Part N：" while 40/41/42/44/47 keep "FDE 面試準備指南（四十N）：RKK 實戰——", and 49 is "FDE Interview Guide Part 49".

### Depth (sourced vs invented-looking numbers, flip conditions, "when it breaks")
- Numbers are stated with precision they do not have: "Red Team 結果，2025 業界報告: 62% / 41% / < 3%" (45:45–48, no report named; and Phase 1 then uses 62% although it includes the regex layer that the same table says gives 41%); "F1 = 0.91 vs 0.74" (47:612); "spaCy zh_core_web_lg PERSON 98.2%" (47:401); "Recall@5 68% → 89%" (44:611); "懶惰評分機率降低 40%（實測）" (50:645). The one honest form — "示意估算，非實測結果" — appears in 43:739, 44:740, 47:606, 48:634, 49:666, 51:684 but is attached to the 系統效應 table only, never to the Phase costs it contradicts.
- Cost models do not close: 43 Phase 1 "$50/月 (n2-standard-4)" (43:76; ≈ $100–140 real); 44 Phase 2 $45K → Phase 3 $15M for 4× users (44:180, 257); 45 Phase costs $50/$300/$2,500 vs 系統效應 $1,800/$3,500/$6,200 (45:97, 166, 224 vs 551–552); 47 three A100 prices in one post ($800/月, $6,400/4 nodes, $3.50/hr ⇒ $2,555/月) (47:103, 255, 439); 48 $380 vs $180 (48:246, 625); 49 Phase 2 $183 for a sum of $133 (49:183); 51 Flash compaction $10,800/月 in 七 vs $2,160 in 九 for the same "18 萬次/天" (51:662, 729).
